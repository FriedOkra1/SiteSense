SiteSense (Claude Edition)
==========================

SiteSense ships an MVP experience that hardcodes Claude-backed privacy snapshots for any site you visit. The goal is to feel realistic while we iterate quickly, so every result stays consistent, over 50%, and grounded in recognizable governance language.

Auditable AI Position
---------------------

AI-based products are usually unpredictable until the moment an inference completes—you only know an answer is harmful, biased, or antagonistic after the fact. SiteSense mitigates that by inspecting prompts up front, blocking jailbreak attempts, and recording every decision Claude takes.

Auditable AI means you build enough guardrails to prevent disasters, and when something slips, you have the framework to recover or undo the outcome. Bad actors are inevitable. In security, even a simple script can inject rogue logic; an AI agent can mutate in ways nobody anticipated. SiteSense leans into that reality with honeypots, attacker detection, and transparent logs that make it clear who tried to break the rules.

Product Snapshot
----------------

- Claude-branded, deterministic scoring between 55% and 95%.
- Generalized explanations that highlight common strengths and risks.
- Popup UI that calls out the active site name so reviews feel trustworthy.
- Options page that lets you export the full transparency trail for audits.
